Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories

Fusion Calculator and More!

Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories is a terrible game with an AWESOME mechanic known as "fusions".
Fusions allow players to mash cards together in an effort to create powerful cards.

Solumin and co. have created a powerful Fusion Calculator!
KAMIGENO has expanded on this GREATLY.
Please check out the new features!

 FUSION SEARCH 

With "Fusion Search"... you can look up any card!
Its information will appear on the right of your screen.

"Search by NAME" will show you what your inputted card can fuse into.
"Search by RESULT" will show you what fuses into your inputted card.
