 ABOUT 

PLEASE NOTE: This "site" was not fully set-up by KAMIGENO.
A lot of the ground-work was already set up... but KAMIGENO changed and tweaked a lot of aspects.
The main "feature" (and purpose) of this site is an expanded "card hand" of up to 800 cards... alongside a "fusion statistics" page.
Given her autism... KAMIGENO added some pages... alongside touching up some "aesthetics" and information.
 
Thank Solumin and their team, by the way.
Pretty much every single thing KAMIGENO did has at least some basis on what they did... more or less.
 

Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories is a terrible game with an AWESOME mechanic known as "fusions".
Fusions allow players to mash cards together in an effort to create powerful cards.
 
Since YGO:FM is a terrible game... it has no in-game tutorial telling you about fusions.
Because 1 card might be able to fuse with 100+ different cards—or even itself—trying to find out what is feasible and useful is tedious.

Solumin's real motivation for this project is Giver336's LP of the game on Something Awful.
His co-commentator General Yeti mused about the possibility of a program to find the fusions for you.
Here it is!
 
As for KAMIGENO's involvement... she just wanted to test out stuff involving the initial deck FM starts players with.
The currently existing tools did not meet her needs... so... here we are!
